# Ruan Valente

### Software Engineer · Frontend Engineering · AI Engineering

Belém, Pará, Brazil

Software Engineer with 5+ years of experience building modern, scalable, and product-oriented software.

My background is strongly rooted in Frontend Engineering and Software Architecture, working on medium and large-scale applications throughout their lifecycle — from technical decisions and architecture to implementation, testing, maintenance, and continuous evolution in production.

My core expertise is in React, Next.js, Angular, Vue.js, and TypeScript, with experience in Design Systems, Micro Frontends, accessibility, performance, automated testing, and scalable frontend architectures.

I am currently expanding my focus toward AI Engineering, exploring LLMs, AI Agents, and Agentic Workflows, with a particular interest in how AI can become part of software engineering and product development.
